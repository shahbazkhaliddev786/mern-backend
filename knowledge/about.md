# About Aljo Store

## Who is Aljo Store?

Aljo Store was established in 2020. It sells "slow goods": pieces made with independent weavers, ceramicists and generational makers, in small batches, and built to last rather than to follow trends.

<!-- source: About page (mern-frontend/src/features/aljo-store/components/about-section.tsx, value-section.tsx) -->

## What materials do you use?

Natural materials with untreated finishes that age well, such as unbleached organic linen, heavy-drape cotton, vegetable-tanned hides and raw stoneware.

<!-- source: About page (about-section.tsx, value-section.tsx) -->

## How are your products made?

In modest, limited batches with independent workshops and generational craftspeople who use traditional techniques and are paid fair wages. Every piece is checked seam by seam before it leaves the workshop.
